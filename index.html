01 / GAMEPLAYMISSION PARAMETERS

FAST REFLEXES.
SMARTER CHOICES.

A vertical space shooter with an entire universe of decisions beyond the battlefield.

Steer to survive, fire automatically, and unleash Overdrive when it counts. Between flights, choose your pilot, tune weapons and power-ups, and plan the next mission.

01 / STEER

TAKE CONTROL

Move side to side through enemy fire. Your weapons auto-fire, leaving you to focus on movement, timing, and staying alive.
